Privacy Policy

Our commitment to your spiritual privacy.

❤️
Our Mission: Seva

Bhakti Marg is offered as a selfless digital Seva. To ensure this platform remains accessible, we operate on a strict Zero-Cost Infrastructure. Your spiritual journey is exclusively yours.

1
100% Local Storage

We do not own or operate external databases. Your daily Japs, sacred notes, and milestones are saved strictly within your device's internal memory. We cannot see, read, or sell your personal spiritual data.

2
Google Drive Backups

If you enable Cloud Sync, the app creates a secure backup file in a hidden folder inside your personal Google Drive. The app uses a restricted scope and can ONLY access the specific backup file it creates. We cannot read your emails or view your personal photos.

3
Push Notifications

We utilize Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver silent updates to your device (such as notifying the app that a new Daily Darshan is available). Firebase generates an anonymous instance ID to route these messages. We do not link this ID to your personal identity.

4
Ads & Analytics

We use Google AdMob to keep the app free. These services may collect anonymous device identifiers and crash logs to serve relevant ads and improve app stability. This data is handled exclusively by Google and is not linked to your personal identity. You can manage ad personalization in your Android OS settings.

5
Your Right to Deletion

You have full control over your data. By navigating to your Profile and selecting 'Delete All Data', the app will instantly erase all local records and permanently remove the backup file from your Google Drive.

This action cannot be undone.
