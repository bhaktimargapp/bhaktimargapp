Privacy Policy

Our commitment to your spiritual privacy.

❤️
Our Mission: Seva

Bhakti Marg is offered as a selfless digital Seva. To ensure this platform remains accessible, we operate on a strict Zero-Cost Infrastructure. Your spiritual journey is exclusively yours.

1
100% Local Storage

Your daily Japs, sacred notes, and milestones are stored strictly within your device's internal memory. We do not operate proprietary backend servers to harvest or read your personal spiritual data.

2
Google Drive Cloud Sync

If you choose to enable the Cloud Backup feature, Bhakti Marg will create a secure, encrypted backup file in a hidden, app-specific directory within your personal Google Drive account. The app requests restricted scope access meaning it can only access the specific file it creates. It cannot read your personal files, emails, or photos.

3
Push Notifications (FCM)

We utilize Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver silent updates to your device (such as notifying the app that a new Daily Darshan is available). Firebase generates an anonymous instance ID to route these messages. We do not link this ID to your personal identity.

4
Third-Party Services & Advertising

To sustain our zero-cost infrastructure, Bhakti Marg integrates Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's Advertising ID exclusively for advertising and analytics. This data is handled exclusively by Google and is not linked to any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) by our application. You can manage your ad personalization preferences in your Android OS settings.

5
Data Deletion Instructions

As per Google Play Developer Policies, you have the absolute right to delete your data at any time. Because your data is stored locally and on your personal Google Drive, you have complete control over its destruction.

To delete your data: Open the Bhakti Marg app > Navigate to the Profile Tab > Scroll to the bottom > Tap "Delete All Data".

This action will permanently wipe your local device database and securely delete the backup file from your Google Drive. This action cannot be undone.

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us at: bhaktimargapp@gmail.com
