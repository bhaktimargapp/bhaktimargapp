App Guide

Master your digital spiritual journey.

📿
The Digital Mala Engine
The Mala screen registers a Jap anywhere you tap on the screen. To help you maintain inner focus without looking at your phone, the device provides a haptic vibration per tap. Upon reaching the 108 threshold (one full Mala), the vibration pattern changes distinctly.
🎯
Naam Anushthan (Sankalp)
Set a dedicated chanting goal (e.g., 1 Lakh Japs) over a specific duration. The app calculates your required daily pace and tracks your progress through a dynamic visual ring on the Sankalp dashboard.
🗺️
Tirth Darshan & Manasi Puja
Explore the sacred lands of Braj through an interactive map. Tap on any location marker to listen to offline spiritual audios associated with that tirth. You can also perform digital Manasi Puja right from your screen.
📅
Spiritual Calendar & Heatmap
Access the authentic Vrindavan Panchang completely offline. The calendar also functions as a visual heatmap, darkening the dates where you chanted more, allowing you to track your devotion over time.
🖼️
Instant Darshan Updates
We utilize a highly optimized edge network for Daily Darshan. The app does not constantly drain your battery checking the internet. Instead, you receive a silent push notification that prepares the images instantly.
⏰
Smart Reminders & Alarms
Customize your interval frequency and active hours (IST) to receive timely devotional prompts throughout the day. Unselected hour blocks automatically respect your Do Not Disturb preferences.
🔄
Auto-Healing Streaks & Vault
Your daily streak updates automatically at Midnight. If you acquire a new phone or accidentally uninstall, simply connect your Google Account in the Cloud Vault tab to restore your entire timeline and diary notes.
📖
Managing Scriptures & Paath
The Paath library organizes sacred texts into categories. You can download texts from the cloud, import your own PDFs, or view your saved records. Our reader is optimized with bookmarking capabilities.
📱
Home Screen Widgets
Long-press your device's home screen to add the 'Quick Mala' and 'Daily Panchang' widgets. This allows you to track Japs and check Tithi/Muhurtas instantly without opening the app.
