Master your digital spiritual journey.

🖼️
Instant Darshan Updates

We utilize a highly optimized edge network for Daily Darshan. The app does not constantly drain your battery checking the internet. Instead, when a new Darshan is uploaded, you receive a silent push notification that prepares the images for you instantly.

📿
The Digital Mala Engine

The Mala screen registers a Jap anywhere you tap on the screen. To help you maintain inner focus without looking at your phone, the device provides a subtle haptic vibration per tap. Upon reaching the 108 threshold, the vibration pattern changes distinctly.

🔥
Auto-Healing Streaks

Your daily streak updates automatically at Midnight. If you acquire a new phone, simply connect your Google Account in the Cloud Vault tab. Our system will download your backup and restore your timeline, ensuring your unbroken devotion continues.

🔒
App PIN & Security

If you forget your App PIN, tapping 'Forgot PIN' prompts a Biometric check. If no biometrics are set up, it will trigger an Emergency Factory Reset to protect your privacy. You can then safely restore your data from your Cloud Vault.

📖
Managing Scriptures

The 'My Paath' library organizes sacred texts into categories. You can download texts directly or upload your own local PDFs. Our engine is highly optimized, but exceptionally large PDFs may take a moment to load depending on your device's memory.

☁️
Background Cloud Sync

If Cloud Vault is active, the app attempts to silently secure your data every 24 hours. However, strict Battery Savers (like those on Xiaomi or Samsung devices) may delay this background sync until you wake up and unlock your phone in the morning.
