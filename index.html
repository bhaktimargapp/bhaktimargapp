Instant Darshan Updates

We utilize a zero-drain edge network for Daily Darshan. The app does not constantly check the internet in the background. Instead, when a new Darshan is uploaded, you receive a silent push notification that updates the images instantly the moment you open the app, saving your battery.

📿
The Digital Mala Engine

The Mala screen registers a Jap anywhere you tap on the screen. To help you maintain inner focus, the device provides a subtle haptic vibration per tap. Upon reaching the 108 threshold (one full Mala), the vibration pattern changes distinctly so you know a round is complete without opening your eyes.

🔥
Auto-Healing Streaks

Your daily streak updates automatically at Midnight. If you acquire a new phone, simply connect your Google Account in the Profile tab. Our system will download your vault and restore your timeline, ensuring your unbroken devotion continues seamlessly.

🔒
App PIN & Security

If you forget your App PIN, tapping 'Forgot PIN' prompts a Biometric (Fingerprint/Face) check. If no biometrics are set up on your device, it will trigger an Emergency Factory Reset. This protects your privacy by clearing the app data, allowing you to restore it fresh from your Cloud Vault.

📖
Managing Scriptures

The 'My Paath' library organizes sacred texts into categories. You can download texts directly or upload your own PDFs. Our engine is highly optimized, but very large PDFs may take a moment to load depending on your device's memory.

☁️
Background Cloud Sync

If Cloud Vault is active, the app attempts to silently secure your data every 24 hours. However, strict Battery Savers (like those on Xiaomi or Samsung devices) may delay this background sync until you wake up and unlock your phone in the morning.
